MyPoster

A personal scheduling tool for sharing gaming videos.

MyPoster lets a creator upload their own finished short videos (Minecraft, Roblox and Fortnite) and publish or schedule them to their own TikTok and YouTube accounts from one place.

  • Connect your TikTok or YouTube account with the platform's official sign-in.
  • Upload a video, write the caption, and choose privacy, comment, Duet and Stitch settings.
  • Preview the post, then post now or pick a time.

Nothing is ever posted without the account owner choosing to post it.
