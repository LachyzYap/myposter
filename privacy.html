Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains what MyPoster ("the service"), run by Lachyz, collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Account details from TikTok and YouTube when you connect an account: your display name, username, profile picture and an access token. With TikTok this comes through TikTok Login Kit (scopes such as user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish).
  • Content you upload: videos, captions, titles, tags and the post settings you choose.
  • Post results: post IDs, links and basic public stats (such as views) so you can see how your posts did.

How we use it

  • To show which account you are posting to.
  • To publish or schedule your videos to the accounts you connected, with the settings you chose.
  • To show you your post history and performance.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the platform you are posting to (TikTok or YouTube).

Storage and security

Data is stored on a private server controlled by the service owner. Access tokens are stored securely and are only used to make the requests you ask for.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept while your account is connected. When you disconnect an account, its tokens are deleted. You can ask for all your data to be deleted at any time by messaging @.lachyz on TikTok. You can also remove the service's access in your TikTok settings (Security and permissions → Apps) or at Google account permissions.

Google and YouTube

The service uses YouTube API Services. By connecting YouTube you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Use of information from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

The service is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: message @.lachyz on TikTok.
